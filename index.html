A little about me

02

I'm a computer science student building a foundation in design, code, and creative problem solving.

I enjoy turning an open-ended question into a clear visual direction, then bringing it to life with HTML and CSS. I'm especially curious about interfaces, typography, and the small details that make digital experiences feel welcoming. I care about both how something works and how it makes people feel while using it.

I've been learning at Mind Do AI for a while, where I've been developing my understanding of technology, artificial intelligence, and how to approach new problems with curiosity. That experience has encouraged me to keep experimenting, ask better questions, and build a stronger technical foundation.

As I continue studying computer science, I'm looking for opportunities to learn from generous teams at ambitious companies. I hope to contribute as someone who brings an eagerness to learn, an eye for clear design, and the patience to improve an idea one detail at a time. This site will grow as I ship more work.
